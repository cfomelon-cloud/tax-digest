China (Mainland) — offshore-asset campaign

🇨🇳 Update / continued coverage Wealthy investors move from shock to reassessment

  • Reported · reputable press Reuters analysis — "China tax crackdown forces wealthy investors to assess their offshore trusts" (21 Aug). Nearly a month after Announcement 2026 No. 21 introduced the flat 20% individual income tax on offshore-trust settlement gains and annual trust income, Reuters (Summer Zhen, Selena Li, Xinghui Kok) documents how the mainland ultra-wealthy are responding in practice. Boston Consulting Group puts roughly US$1.2 trillion of Chinese HNW money in low-tax jurisdictions, and more than half of China's super-rich are said to use offshore family trusts. Advisers report clients unwinding trusts — especially founders preparing Hong Kong listings — holding off on new structures in favour of smaller offshore managers, and even liquidating A-shares or borrowing to meet bills when wealth is locked in illiquid property. Singapore lawyer Ryan Lin said more than half of his 50-plus trust clients now plan to declare and pay, bluntly framing the choice as "declare, or don't declare and unwind." The looming pressure point remains the 90-day disclosure window to regularise unpaid tax on assets settled into trusts since January 2023 and pre-2026 trust income. Bank of America analysts warn enforcement could next reach overseas employment income and offshore share-trading gains as provincial finances strain under the property downturn; the near-term read is a possible slowing of southbound flows into Hong Kong and other Asian wealth hubs. Taipei Times (Reuters) · Yahoo Finance (Reuters)
Adviser read. This is the first solid, source-rich picture of client behaviour rather than policy mechanics — useful for calibrating your own conversations. The signal for advisers: the 90-day clock, not the headline rate, is what is driving decisions, and the "unwind vs. declare" fork is now being made case-by-case. Two practical cautions. First, unwinding a trust is itself a taxable and often irreversible event — model the settlement-stage and liquidation charges (and the three-year establishment-stage exemption) before acting. Second, the Bank of America expansion thesis (salary, offshore trading gains) is analyst forecast, not announced policy — flag it to clients as a planning scenario, not a rule. Keep gathering establishment-date and cost-base records now, while the disclosure route is open.

Hong Kong — offshore insurance

🇭🇰 Update / continued coverage First sales data since the insurance-tax scare

  • Confirmed · corporate results AIA first-half 2026 results — mainland demand holds up (20 Aug). AIA reported record first-half new business value of US$3.21 billion, up 10% year on year, with first-half profit up 11%, led by Hong Kong and wider Asia. On the tax question, regional chief executive Jacky Chan said Beijing's May move requiring source-of-funds declarations and the subsequent reports of a 20% levy on offshore-insurance returns have not dented demand from mainland visitors: "Since May, when news was released regarding controls and tax levies, we haven't seen any change in demand." Second-quarter sales to mainland customers in Hong Kong were said to be stronger than the first quarter, with June the best month, which Chan attributed to mainlanders' appetite to diversify globally, insurance included. This is the first hard, issuer-level datapoint against the August sell-off in Hong Kong insurers. South China Morning Post
Adviser read. The market's fear in early August was that the 20% policy-returns levy would choke the mainland-to-Hong Kong insurance flow; AIA's numbers say that had not happened by mid-year. It squares with S&P Global Ratings' 14 August view that Hong Kong life insurers should still see 8–10% annual premium growth despite the tax, with demand for multi-currency diversification and protection "intact" (SCMP, 14 Aug). Two caveats for client conversations: AIA's window largely predates the sharpest enforcement headlines of August, so it is a lagging read; and resilient sales do not remove the individual policyholder's exposure — a mainland-resident client still owes 20% IIT on taxable policy returns (cash dividends and interest on prepaid/advance premiums) regardless of how buoyant the sales league tables look. Advise on the client's own reporting position, not the sector's momentum.

  • 已披露 · 可靠媒體 路透分析——〈中國稅務稽查迫使富裕投資人重新檢視其離岸信託〉（8月21日）。在《2026年第21號公告》就離岸信託設立所得與存續期間所得引入單一20%個人所得稅近一個月後，路透（Summer Zhen、Selena Li、Xinghui Kok）記錄大陸超高淨值人士的實際回應。波士頓顧問公司（BCG）估計約有1.2兆美元中國高淨值資金置於低稅法域，且逾半數中國超級富豪據稱使用離岸家族信託。顧問反映客戶正拆解信託（尤以籌備香港上市的創辦人為甚）、暫緩設立新架構而轉向規模較小的離岸資產管理機構，甚至在財富被不動產鎖住時出售A股或借款以支應稅款。新加坡律師Ryan Lin表示，其逾50名信託客戶中已有過半計畫申報並繳稅，並直白地將選擇歸結為「申報，或不申報而拆解」。迫近的壓力點仍是90天披露期，用以就2023年1月以來置入信託之資產及2026年前之信託所得補繳未繳稅款。美銀分析師警告，隨地方財政在房市下行下承壓，稽查下一步可能延伸至境外受僱所得與離岸證券交易收益；近期研判為南向流入香港及其他亞洲財富中心的資金可能放緩。Taipei Times（路透） · Yahoo Finance（路透）
