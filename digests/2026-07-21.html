Developments

🇬🇧 United Kingdom Material development

  • Confirmed Electricity VAT cut 5%→0% for six months. Burnham announced removal of VAT on domestic electricity across GB (with equivalent NI funding) from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027, as cost-of-living relief. Independent analysis (Martin Lewis) put the real-terms benefit at roughly £20 over the period, as a ~3% price-cap rise lands at the same time. Costed at around £0.85bn.
    Sources: ITV News · MoneySavingExpert (analysis)
  • Confirmed HMRC inheritance-tax receipts hit record £2.3bn. HMRC data showed IHT receipts of £2.3bn for the three months to June 2026 — a record for the period, up £96m year-on-year — with frozen nil-rate bands dragging more estates into the net. Stamp-duty receipts were also up.
    Sources: CityAM · HMRC receipts bulletin (GOV.UK)
Adviser read: Little direct HNW impact, but note it as a spending commitment that widens the autumn Budget funding gap — reinforcing the case for revenue-raisers elsewhere.
Adviser read: Fiscal drag on estates is accelerating; combined with the April-2027 pensions-into-IHT change and the £1m BPR/APR cap, this strengthens the case for reviewing gifting, trusts and life cover now.
