Top cross-border moves. The UK has a new government (PM Andy Burnham, Chancellor John Healey, from 20 July) already signalling autumn tax rises to fill a ~£22bn gap — the most active private-client story this week. In the US, the OBBBA's permanent $15m estate/gift exemption took effect from 1 January 2026. China's tax authority is actively enforcing a 20% charge on residents' offshore trading gains. Hong Kong has tabled a bill to widen its family-office 0% regime, and Singapore's Budget 2026 withheld the usual personal-tax rebate.

Priority jurisdictions

🇬🇧 United Kingdom Active now

Tracking: income tax & frozen personal allowance (fiscal drag) · CGT (rates, death-uplift, BADR) · IHT — pensions-into-IHT (Apr 2027), BPR/APR £1m cap (Apr 2026), NRB/RNRB freezes · non-dom abolition & 4-year FIG regime, TRF · residence/SRT · property (mansion-tax surcharge, SDLT) · trusts · pensions & ISAs.
  • Reported New Burnham/Healey government (from 20 Jul) faces a ~£22bn gap; independent economists call autumn tax rises "pretty much guaranteed." Burnham has already walked back his suggestion to unfreeze the £12,570 personal allowance. CityAM · Bloomberg
  • Confirmed Unused pensions fall into IHT from 6 April 2027 (Finance Act 2026); BPR/APR 100% relief capped at a combined £1m from 6 April 2026 (excess relieved at 50%, effective 20%). GOV.UK
  • Confirmed "Mansion tax" surcharge on £2m+ homes (£2,500–£7,500/yr) from April 2028; a £1.5m threshold is under discussion (would nearly double the ~127,000 homes in scope). Commons Library
Adviser read: plan on the freeze holding and on CGT, IHT reliefs and property being the Budget's revenue engine. The pre-Budget window (late Nov) favours acting early on disposals and gifting; stress-test any strategy relying on the CGT death-uplift.

Flow: unusually active (new government); normally clusters around the Budget. Sources: GOV.UK/HMRC, IFS, CIOT, Tax Policy Associates, FT/BBC/Reuters.

Flow: high — IRS rulings, §7520 rates, Tax Court and state residency changes year-round. Sources: IRS/Treasury, Tax Notes, Bloomberg Tax, ACTEC, big-4 & law-firm private-client alerts.

🇸🇬 Singapore Episodic

Tracking: personal income tax & residency · no CGT / no estate duty · remitted foreign income · family-office schemes 13O/13U/13D (MAS AUM, spending & local-investment rules; sunset 31 Dec 2029) · GIP residency · VCC · property tax & ABSD (incl. 65% trust ABSD) · philanthropy incentives · trusts, CRS, Pillar Two.

Flow: episodic, spikes around the Feb Budget. Sources: IRAS, MAS, MOF, big-4 SG alerts, Business Times, Straits Times.
