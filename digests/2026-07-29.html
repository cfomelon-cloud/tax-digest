🇨🇳 China (Mainland) — 20% offshore-trust income tax Update / continued coverage

  • Reported · professional press The Accountant: "China targets offshore trusts with new tax rules" (28 Jul). Professional-press write-up of MOF / State Taxation Administration Announcement 2026 No.21: individual income tax on gains when assets (shares, real estate) are first placed into an offshore trust, plus annual tax on trust income going forward, and a 90-day voluntary-disclosure window for 2023–2025 transfers. Frames the move as closing a long-used HNW loophole under fiscal pressure, and notes Hong Kong has overtaken Switzerland as the largest offshore-wealth hub. The Accountant Online
  • Reported · Chinese-language RFA (中文): enforcement and retroactivity in focus (28 Jul). Mandarin-language report emphasising the compliance mechanics: residents must complete back-filing within 90 days for trusts established 2023–2025 and for pre-2026 income; late-payment charges and fines apply to non-compliers; and the tax authorities may extend the limitation period where unpaid amounts are large. Trust income is reportable annually whether or not distributed, with no deduction for trust-management expenses. RFA Mandarin
  • Trade press Continued coverage. Global Private Banker (27 Jul) restates the 20% charge on offshore-trust asset appreciation and annual income for Chinese-resident settlors/beneficiaries, flagging the Hong-Kong-structure angle. Global Private Banker
Adviser read: the story has shifted from scope to enforcement. For any Chinese-tax-resident settlor, beneficiary or protector of an offshore (often Hong Kong / BVI / Cayman) trust, the near-term action items are concrete: confirm whether a 2023–2025 establishment or pre-2026 income triggers the 90-day back-filing clock, quantify the 20% exposure across establishment / operation / liquidation, and document positions before the extended-limitation risk bites.
