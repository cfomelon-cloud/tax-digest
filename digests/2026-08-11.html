Adviser read. The signal today is framing, not a new rule: reputable press is now treating the trust tax, the insurance-return tax and the 2026 broking penalties as one campaign to bring mainland residents' offshore income and assets inside the individual-income-tax net, all riding on CRS data. Three practical takeaways. First, widen the review beyond trusts and policies to offshore brokerage/fund holdings — the Futu/Tiger/Longbridge actions were against platforms, but they signal the same information trail that reaches an individual's overseas securities gains and dividends; map where a client's mainland-resident nexus meets each asset class. Second, the 香港中通社 clarification is genuinely useful at the client table: on insurance, the charge bites the return leg (dividends, prepaid-premium interest), while protection payouts (death / critical-illness / medical) and unrealised account value are not the target — so illustrations should be re-run on an after-tax basis rather than abandoned. Third, keep the trust 90-day voluntary-disclosure clock (to ~21 October) on the calendar for any client with a Chinese-resident settlor, beneficiary or controlling party. The "does Hong Kong win or lose" debate is noise for planning purposes; the constant is that transparency, not the choice of wrapper, now drives exposure. Big-4 and specialist HK/PRC firm alerts putting numbers on the securities and insurance mechanics are still awaited.

顧問解讀。今日之訊號在於框架，而非新規則：可靠媒體如今將信託稅、保險收益稅與2026年之經紀處罰視為同一波行動，旨在將內地居民之境外所得與資產納入個人所得稅網，全部以CRS資料為依托。三項實務要點。其一，將檢視範圍自信託與保單擴及離岸券商／基金持倉——富途／老虎／長橋之處罰針對平台，但其揭示之資訊軌跡，同樣可及於個人之境外證券收益與股息；請盤點客戶之內地居民連結與各資產類別之交會處。其二，香港中通社之澄清於客戶對話中確有助益：就保險而言，課稅落在收益段（分紅、預繳保費利息），而保障給付（身故／重疾／醫療）及未實現帳戶價值並非課稅對象——故示例應改以稅後基礎重算，而非全然放棄。其三，就任何具中國稅務居民委託人、受益人或控制方之客戶，將信託90天自願披露期限（至約10月21日）記入行事曆。「香港究竟得利或受損」之爭論就規劃而言屬雜訊；不變者是——如今驅動風險者為透明度，而非架構工具之選擇。四大及香港／大陸專業事務所就證券與保險計算機制給出具體數字之快訊，仍在等待中。
