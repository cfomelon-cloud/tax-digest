China (Mainland) / Hong Kong — offshore-insurance income tax

🇨🇳🇭🇰 Update / continued coverage Mainland press weighs the tax-plus-FX squeeze on Hong Kong policy demand

  • Reputable press · explainer Beijing Business Today (北京商报) — "Caught between currency swings and tax rumours, is buying Hong Kong insurance cooling off?" (9 Aug). A mainland financial-daily assessment that moves the story from market panic to client economics. It restates the official line — the Hong Kong Insurance Authority and State Taxation Administration both say the obligation to declare and pay IIT on overseas investment returns "has always existed" and should not be over-interpreted — then quantifies the drag. On the illustration used, a 20-year savings policy projected at 6% would return roughly 5.2% net once the 20% tax bites on the return element; separately, the renminbi's ~4.2% rise against the dollar in 2025 reduces the home-currency value of maturity/withdrawal proceeds. Context figures: mainland visitors bought about HK$62.8bn of new Hong Kong policies in 2024, ~30% of new-business volume; analysts flag a possible ~10% hit to volumes. The counterweight the paper stresses: Hong Kong participating/savings products still illustrate at 6–7% versus roughly 3% for comparable onshore products, so demand may soften rather than disappear. 北京商报 Beijing Business Today (Chinese)
Adviser read. This is the first piece to put usable numbers on the "so what" for policyholders, and it reframes the client conversation around net outcomes rather than headlines. Two practical points. First, the tax and the currency move are separate, cumulative effects: the 20% IIT bites on the return/growth leg, while the RMB's appreciation hits anyone converting USD/HKD proceeds back to renminbi — model both when reviewing an illustration, and don't let a client conflate them. Second, the comparison that matters to mainland buyers is still Hong Kong ~6–7% (pre-tax illustration) versus ~3% onshore, so even at a ~5.2% net illustration the offshore product can remain competitive for genuine long-horizon savers — the planning question is suitability and disclosure, not a blanket exit. Keep separating protection cover (death / critical-illness / medical payouts remain exempt) from the taxable return leg, gather premium-cost and distribution records now to compute any taxable gain, and read this alongside the offshore-trust regime (same 20% rate, same CRS enabler, disclosure window to ~21 Oct). Big-4 and specialist HK/PRC firm alerts firming up the calculation mechanics are still awaited.

🇨🇳🇭🇰 最新／後續報導 大陸媒體評估稅負加匯率對香港保單需求之雙重擠壓

  • 可靠媒體 · 解說 北京商報 —〈遭匯率波動、稅收傳聞雙重「夾擊」，赴港投保要降溫了嗎〉（8月9日）。一篇大陸財經日報之評析，將議題自市場恐慌轉向客戶實質經濟效益。文中先重申官方口徑——香港保險業監管局與國家稅務總局均稱，居民就境外投資收益申報並繳納個稅之義務「一直存在」、不應過度解讀——繼而量化其拖累。以所用示例計，一份預期6%之20年期儲蓄保單，於收益部分課徵20%後淨報酬約為5.2%；另外，人民幣2025年兌美元升值約4.2%，降低到期／提領給付換回本幣後之價值。脈絡數據：2024年內地訪客購買香港新保單約628億港元、約佔新造業務量30%；分析預估保單量或受約10%衝擊。該報強調之抗衡因素：香港分紅／儲蓄保單仍以6–7%示例，相較同類境內產品約3%，故需求或轉趨疲軟而非消失。北京商報（中文）
顧問解讀。此為首篇就「所以呢」對投保人給出可用數字之報導，並將客戶對話重新框定於淨結果而非標題。兩個實務要點。其一，稅負與匯率變動屬獨立且可累加之效果：20%個稅落在收益／增值段，而人民幣升值則影響任何將美元／港元給付換回人民幣者——檢視示例時二者皆須試算，勿讓客戶混為一談。其二，對內地買家真正重要之比較，仍是香港約6–7%（稅前示例）對境內約3%，故縱使淨示例約5.2%，對真正長期儲蓄者而言離岸產品仍可具競爭力——規劃課題在於適合度與揭露，而非全面退場。持續將保障給付（身故／重疾／醫療給付仍免稅）與應稅收益段分開，即刻蒐集保費成本與派發紀錄以計算應稅收益，並將此與離岸信託制度（稅率同為20%、同以CRS為基礎，披露期至約10月21日）併同檢視。四大及香港／大陸專業事務所就計算機制發布更明確快訊，仍在等待中。
