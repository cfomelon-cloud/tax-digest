  • Confirmed · in force The regime is live; the disclosure clock keeps ticking. The mechanics carried in earlier editions are unchanged: a Chinese tax resident is taxed at a flat 20% at three points — settlement into the trust (on the transfer gain), annually on trust income whether or not distributed, and on liquidation; foreign taxes are creditable, income already taxed on distribution is not taxed twice, trusts running three or more years are exempt from retroactive establishment-stage tax, and a 90-day voluntary-disclosure window (from 24 July, closing ~21 October) lets residents regularise unreported 2023–2025 amounts without late-payment surcharges. Taiwan — succession & estate tax reform

🇹🇼 Update / continued coverage — two reforms on parallel tracks, no new in-window article

  • Reported Estate & Gift Tax Act amendment (anti-disclaimer / spousal clause) — with the Legislature. The Executive Yuan's draft (approved 30 July) reallocates who bears estate tax on pre-death gifts: recipients of gifts made within two years of death must bear estate tax on those assets in proportion to their share even if they disclaim the inheritance, killing the "disclaim-and-shift" tactic, while a taxpayer-favourable change lets qualifying pre-death gifts count toward the spousal deduction. It has been referred to the Legislative Yuan; no effective date is set, and no committee reading was reported in the window.
  • Confirmed · background Civil Code art. 1223 — siblings' forced-heirship reserved portion abolished. The Legislature passed the amendment in late July; it takes effect six months after presidential promulgation and removes only the forced-heirship protection for siblings (previously one-third of a sibling's statutory portion), leaving their intestate succession rights (art. 1138) intact. Background and firm analysis remain: Focus Taiwan (28 Jul) · Taipei Times (29 Jul) · Celsum Attorneys explainer (28 Jul), with the 2 August Taipei Times editorial the most recent commentary.
Adviser read. The planning points are unchanged and worth restating because both reforms reward action now, before effective dates land. For large intra-family transfers, assume the intended recipient (not the residual heirs) will carry the proportional estate tax if death falls within two years, and model the spousal-deduction detail where a spouse is the donee. On the civil-law side, the widened testamentary freedom most benefits childless couples and those wishing to favour a charity or non-family heir over siblings — but only through a valid, carefully drafted, internationally-coordinated will; absent one, statutory succession still applies. Fix two dates: confirm the presidential promulgation date (to start the six-month Civil Code clock) and track Legislative Yuan passage of the Estate & Gift Tax Act amendment.

台灣 — 繼承與遺產稅改革

🇹🇼 更新／後續報導 — 兩項改革並行，期間內無新文章

  • 已披露 《遺產及贈與稅法》修正（反拋棄／配偶條款）——現於立法院。行政院草案（7月30日核定）重新分配死亡前贈與之遺產稅由誰負擔：於死亡前兩年內受贈財產者，須就該等財產按其應繼份比例負擔遺產稅——即使拋棄繼承亦然，藉此終結「拋棄以轉嫁」之手法；另有一項有利納稅人之調整，使符合條件之死亡前贈與得計入配偶扣除額。該案已送立法院；尚未定生效日期，期間內亦無委員會審查之報導。
  • 已確認 · 背景 《民法》第1223條——廢除兄弟姊妹之特留分強制繼承保障。立法院於7月下旬三讀通過修正；其自總統公布後六個月施行，且僅廢除兄弟姊妹之特留分保障（原為其應繼分之三分之一），並未廢除其法定（無遺囑）繼承權（第1138條）。背景與事務所分析仍為：Focus Taiwan（7月28日） · 《台北時報》（7月29日） · 世勤律師事務所解說（7月28日），並以8月2日《台北時報》社論為最新評論。
顧問解讀。規劃要點不變，值得重申，因兩項改革均獎勵現在採取行動——趕在生效日期到來之前。就大額家族內移轉而言，若死亡發生於兩年內，應假設由預定受贈人（而非剩餘繼承人）承擔按比例之遺產稅，並於配偶為受贈對象時試算配偶扣除額之細節。民事法面，擴大之遺囑自由對無子女之夫妻、以及欲將財產優先留予慈善機構或非家族繼承人而非兄弟姊妹者最為有利——惟須透過有效、審慎草擬並跨境協調之遺囑方能實現；如無遺囑，仍適用法定繼承。應確認之兩項日期：查明總統公布日（以起算民法之六個月施行期），並追蹤《遺產及贈與稅法》修正於立法院之通過。
