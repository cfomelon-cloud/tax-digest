Story tracker — Elborne v HMRC

The Court of Appeal's 13 July 2026 decision upholding a pre-2006 "home loan / double trust" inheritance-tax scheme · living roundup of commentary · last updated 26 July 2026

What happened. On 13 July 2026 the Court of Appeal dismissed HMRC's appeal in Elborne v HMRC, upholding a pre-2006 "home loan / double trust" scheme: the owner sold her home to trustees for a promissory note and gifted the note to a second family settlement, so the trustees' debt depresses the property's value for IHT while she remained in occupation. HMRC's "disregard the debt", gift-with-reservation and "commercial reality over form" arguments all failed. Crucially the scheme pre-dated DOTAS/GAAR — the court indicated GAAR "could in principle have been deployed" today, so modern equivalents remain vulnerable. HMRC may seek permission to appeal to the Supreme Court (viewed as unlikely).
